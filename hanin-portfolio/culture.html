Faith
& Me

I'm Muslim, and my faith shapes how I wake up, how I treat people and how I get through hard days. Islam is the belief in one God and in living with mercy, honesty and gratitude.

For me, culture is not only what we wear or eat. It is the prayer before a meal, the greeting at the door, and the family who always makes room at the table.

“Be kind, be grateful, be present.” What my faith teaches me

Traditions

How it looks at home

Modest Dress

The hijab and flowing dresses are about dignity and comfort. For celebrations, we dress up in rich colors, lace and embroidery.

  • Hijab
  • Eid outfits

Halal Food

We eat halal and begin with Bismillah. Dates break the fast at sunset, and big shared dishes mean nobody eats alone.

  • Halal
  • Iftar
  • Sharing

Daily Prayer

Five prayers a day give the day a rhythm: a pause to slow down, say thank you, and reset.

  • Salah
  • Quran

Eid With Family

Eid mornings start with prayer, then visiting relatives, sharing food and giving gifts to the children.

  • Family
  • Gifts
  • Feast

Words I Use

Bismillah

“In the name of God”

Said before eating, studying or starting anything new.

Beginnings

Alhamdulillah

“Praise be to God”

Said for good news, and also when things are hard, as a reminder to stay grateful.

Gratitude

InshaAllah

“God willing”

Said when talking about plans, because the future is in God's hands.

Hope

Eid Mubarak

“Blessed Eid”

The greeting we share with everyone at the end of Ramadan and during Eid al-Adha.

Celebration
